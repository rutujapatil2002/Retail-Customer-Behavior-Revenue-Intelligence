# Retail-Customer-Behavior-Revenue-Intelligence
This project analyzes retail customer purchasing data to uncover meaningful business insights, understand customer behavior, and evaluate revenue performance. It explores purchasing patterns, product preferences, customer segments, subscription trends, and discount effectiveness to support data-driven business decisions.

-Tools & Technologies:

Python (Pandas): Data cleaning, preprocessing, and feature engineering.

PostgreSQL: Data querying and business analysis using SQL.

Power BI: Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

DAX: Creating measures and calculating key performance indicators.

Excel: Data exploration and analysis.

-Key Analysis Areas

Customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.

Revenue analysis and product performance.

Customer segmentation (New, Returning, and Loyal).

Subscription status and customer spending comparison.

Discount effectiveness across products.

Product ratings and customer preferences.

-Project Objective

To transform raw retail customer data into actionable business insights that help understand customer preferences, identify revenue opportunities, and improve retail business performance.
